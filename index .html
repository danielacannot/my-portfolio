About

I'm a graduate student at Northwestern University's Medill School of Journalism, where I concentrate in Magazine writing. Due to my passion for filmmaking and fine art, I find myself drawn to the curation of opinion pieces about new and retrospective film and Chicagoland entertainment journalism..

Before journalism school, I studied under Emerson College & Paris College of Art's co-degree Global Bachelor of Fine Arts in Film Art program, which gave me a variety of production related skills and a deep appreciation for cinematic art. Coming from a small town in rural Texas, I have an appreciation for highlighting the artistic happenings of filmmakers with small budgets/limited production resources.

I'm drawn to discussions around genre film, as I feel that this category of cinema can draw out some of the unrest harboring in today's society.

Film & Visual Projects

Kansas City (Short Film) (2025)

The film followws Parker and Bianca, two young adults, experience the unraveling of their relationship as Parker develops a deluded sexual obsession with erotic online personality Kansas Fox. As his obsession deepens, he believes she is digitally following him wherever he goes and communicating with him by any means possible. I directed and wrote the film. Kansas City was screened at Emerson College's Paramount Theatre, currently in festival circulation.

Dani, Auntie, Annigoni (Documentary) (2024)

Documentary filmed in Well, Netherlands for a Documentary course at Emerson College's Kasteel Well. The documentary follows my figurative journey of learning about generational othering through art through portraits by Pietro Annigoni and my relationship with my aunt. I served as director and starred in the film.
